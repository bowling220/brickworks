# BRICKWORKS - Audio Credits & Attribution

BRICKWORKS features music and procedural soundscapes designed to create a peaceful, immersive building environment. All musical works and sound effects used in BRICKWORKS adhere to strict Creative Commons licensing and public domain guidelines.

## Sound Effects & Environmental Soundscapes

All environmental soundscapes, ambient loops (breeze, strong wind, daytime birds, nighttime crickets, rain, thunder), water stream sounds, brick placement clicks, blueprint chimes, terraforming feedback, and UI sounds were synthesized procedurally using digital signal processing algorithms by the BRICKWORKS Core Engineering Team and are dedicated to the public domain under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
